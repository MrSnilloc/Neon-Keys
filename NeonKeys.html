POSTURE CHECK
Ten seconds now saves your wrists later. Tap each one.
Bench height: elbows level with the keys
Sit tall on the front half of the bench, feet flat on the floor
Shoulders down, arms heavy: let gravity drop your arm into the keys
Loose hands: fingers relaxed and fairly flat, so you can feel the keys
Breathe: don't hold your breath on the hard parts
GOLDEN RULEPractice should never hurt. If anything aches, stop and rest before you get tired.
